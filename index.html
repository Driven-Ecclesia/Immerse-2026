Why Immerse?

Three days set apart for divine consecration.

Immerse is The Driven Ecclesia's annual retreat. Three days set apart to intensify in prayers, worship and the word. A time of alignment, direction and clarity of purpose.

No distractions, no rush, just space to hear from God and mix with a community of like minded believers.

The first edition was great, and the second edition would be even greater.

What To Expect

Three days, each building on the next.

From arrival to impartation to being sent out — the retreat is structured as a journey, not a schedule.

01

Arrival & Consecration

Check-in, orientation, and an opening night of worship and consecration to set the tone for the retreat.

02

Encounter & The Word

A full day of teaching sessions, extended prayer, and ministration — the heart of the Immerse experience.

03

Commissioning & Sending Forth

Closing communion, testimonies, and a commissioning service before departure.
